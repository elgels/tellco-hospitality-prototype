Hospitality services

A Proven Clean-Energy Platform, Built for Guest-Facing Properties

Hotels and real-estate assets need infrastructure that improves comfort, installs with minimal disruption, performs reliably, and can be measured, maintained, financed, and expanded over time. TellCo brings its clean-energy platform—solar, storage, lighting, water-energy planning, cooling, smart controls, monitoring, EPC coordination, training, and long-term service—to the property environment.

The result is a practical operating layer for hospitality and real estate: lower utility exposure, dependable power for essential systems, better indoor and outdoor environments, safer pathways and parking areas, and evidence for owner, investor, and sustainability reporting.

Net zero program support

Site-Level Evidence for Hospitality Owners, Operators, and Sustainability Leaders

Hotel groups, resort operators, developers, and real-estate investors need more than broad sustainability commitments. ESG leaders, asset managers, and investor-relations teams need property-level evidence before they can prioritize investments, brief stakeholders, or support public claims.

TellCo frames that evidence around energy consumption, solar potential, lighting upgrades, cooling and hot-water demand, battery resilience, utility exposure, monitoring records, service models, and finance-ready infrastructure options—so property teams can select credible first pilots and scale successful programs across a portfolio.
